# JSONTestSuite corpus

These `*.json` files are the `test_parsing/` corpus vendored from
[nst/JSONTestSuite](https://github.com/nst/JSONTestSuite) at commit `1ef36fa01286573e846ac449e8683f8833c5b26a`,
used under the MIT license (see `LICENSE`). File-name prefixes: `y_` must be
accepted, `n_` must be rejected, `i_` is implementation-defined.

Vendored (not submoduled) so CI runs offline and deterministically.
